Camera photometry probe

Measures how a held cube's colours drift under the camera's own exposure and white balance, and what holding them does about it. It samples the mean colour of one small square and writes numbers only: no frame is kept, saved or sent anywhere. Run it from localhost or https; the browser asks for the camera as it does for any page. See docs/testing.md, "Measuring the camera's drift".

1. Camera
Click the picture to put the square on a white sticker (or any one sticker; the same one for every run).
2. Record

Hold the cube with that sticker in the square. Each run is 15 s, with a beep at the start and the end. Do them in this order; a run is labelled by the button.

not locked

Turning: rotate the cube slowly (about a quarter turn a second) so the sticker leaves the square and comes back, as it does in a scan; the numbers that matter are the ones taken while it is in the square, so the run keeps only frames where the square is brighter than (8-bit Y').

3. Results
RunFramesHzY' meanY' range %Y' trend % R/G range %B/G range %B/G trend %exposureTime seencolorTemp seen

Range is (max−min)/mean. Trend is (last 2 s mean − first 2 s mean)/mean: a slow drift, which is what one side's reads suffer while the cube is held.
