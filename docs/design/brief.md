# Design brief

Workstream E1 of the [app experience plan](../app-experience-plan.md).
Status: **draft for Iain's sign-off.** Decisions below marked *(Iain,
2026-10-13)* are his; the rest are proposals.

## What Cubeulator is

Point a camera at a scrambled 3x3 cube, show its six sides, then follow the
turns until it is solved. One app, one shared shell, on Windows, macOS,
Linux, Android and iOS.

## Who it is for

*(Iain, 2026-10-13.)* In this order:

1. **Someone who cannot solve a cube.** They have a scrambled cube and want
   it solved. They do not know any notation and do not want a lesson.
2. **Someone who wants to learn the method.** They want to know what each
   stage is for, so that one day they will not need the app.
3. **Cubers, for a giggle.** They read `R U R' U'` already. They get a
   notation switch and the move list; nothing else is built for them.

What this decides:

| for | the design does |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plain words first. The picture carries the instruction. One turn at a time. Notation is off until asked for |
| 2 | Each of the five stages has a name, one sentence on what it does and one on why. Shown at stage changes during the solve and in full in **Review the solution**, when the hands are free |
| 3 | Settings > Solving > **Show cube notation**. Off by default |

## Where and how it is used

These are the plan's four facts. They are assumptions until E6 tests them
with real people.

| fact | consequence |
|---|---|
| **Both hands are on the cube during the solve.** The device is propped up or lying flat, read from arm's length (60-70 cm, about twice a hand-held distance) | The solve screen has two targets a knuckle can hit and nothing smaller. Instruction type is twice body size. The screen does not sleep |
| **On a phone, scanning is one hand each** | Scanning needs no taps at all: holding a side still is the input. Every control on the scan screen sits in the bottom thumb zone and is at least 56 px high |
| **They do not know notation** | A turn is three plain parts: which side, which way, how far. Direction is always given from where the person is holding the cube |
| **Colour is the subject**, and red, orange and green are where red-green colour vision deficiency bites | Every cube colour carries a mark as well as a hue, and is named in words wherever one colour is meant. No state anywhere is carried by colour alone |

Other conditions of use:

- **Light.** Kitchens, desks, sofas; warm lamps and windows behind. The
  scan screen has to say what is wrong in words, because the person cannot
  see what the camera sees in the colours.
- **Interruptions.** A phone call, a locked screen, a closed laptop lid. A
  solve is 60 to 150 turns and takes minutes. Losing the place is losing
  the cube, so the place is kept.
- **A cube in the hand that drifts from the picture.** One wrong turn and
  nothing matches. The way back is always the same and always offered:
  scan the cube again and carry on from where it is.

## Principles

From the plan, unchanged: never stranded; readable from where the cube is;
say what is happening and what to do next; honest; one system; each
platform's manners.

Added by this phase:

- **The cube never mirrors.** In right-to-left languages the layout
  mirrors; the cube, the camera picture, a side's nine stickers and the
  direction of a turn do not.
- **The picture is the truth.** The cube on screen is the cube in the hand.
  When they differ, the app says how to make them agree, never which one is
  wrong.

## What the first release holds

*(Iain, 2026-10-13: dead buttons out; Review solution in; help and
how-to-hold in; solve history in.)*

| in | notes |
|---|---|
| Scan a cube with the camera | Desktop today. Phones when mobile capture exists (plan decision 8) |
| Enter the colours by hand | Recovery plan R3. Also the only way in on a device whose camera the build cannot use, which is every phone until mobile capture lands |
| Check and correct the colours | R3. Reached from a scan that does not make a valid cube, or by choice |
| Follow the solve, one turn at a time | With the hold instruction on every size, a check at each stage change, and a way back when the cube and the picture disagree |
| Carry on an unfinished solve | After leaving, a lock screen, or the app being closed |
| Review the solution | Step through every turn after solving, with the stage explanations |
| Solve history | Kept on the device. Can be turned off and cleared |
| Help | How it works; holding the cube to scan and to solve; what the words mean; trouble scanning; which cubes work |
| Settings | Language, appearance, colour marks, text size, motion, how turns are advanced, notation, sound, camera, history |
| About, licences, privacy | Third-party notices generated by E0b |
| Eight languages | *(Iain, 2026-10-13.)* English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Hebrew, Arabic. Right-to-left is in the first release, so mirroring is designed and tested now |
| Light and dark themes | Following the system, with an override |

| out | why |
|---|---|
| Telemetry, crash upload, accounts, sharing | Plan decision 7. Keeps the privacy statement one paragraph long |
| Voice commands | No on-device speech recognition that works the same on five platforms; several would send audio off the device. See `screens/solve.md` |
| The camera watching each turn | The best answer to hands-full, and the hardest. Needs free-handling reads (R5). Designed for, not promised |
| Stickerless, white-bodied, picture and non-standard-colour cubes | The reader relies on dark plastic between stickers (recovery plan). Help says so plainly |
| Cube sizes other than 3x3 | - |
| Timed solving, scramble generation, statistics | A different product |
| Cube style, graphics backend, frame-rate display as user settings | Moved to developer builds. They change nothing a person solving a cube needs |
| "Detect hands with AI", "Auto-advance between faces" | They drive `ScanSession`, which no screen starts and R6 removes |

## Decisions taken in this phase

Each is a judgement call, with the reason. Any of them can be reversed
before sign-off.

| # | call | why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The red accent stays as the brand colour, but text and button fills use a darker red (`#B82009` light, `#FF7359` / `#FF5A3D` dark) | The existing `#EC3013` measures 3.76:1 on the paper colour and under its own button label. 4.5:1 is the plan's gate |
| D2 | Cube colours are retuned and each carries a mark (ring, dot, plus, triangle, bar; white is plain). Marks are **on by default** | Measured: today orange and green are 5.4 apart under protanopia, where 10 is the least that is reliably tellable. A setting nobody finds helps nobody |
| D3 | A turn is worded from the holder's point of view: top and bottom go left or right, left and right go up or down, front and back go clockwise or anticlockwise as seen from the front | "Clockwise" for a side you cannot see is where beginners go wrong. This is a wording proposal to the solve lane; what a move *means* stays theirs |
| D4 | A valid scan goes straight to the solve, as it does today. Checking the colours is offered, not imposed | A valid cube is very probably the right cube. Fifty-four stickers to approve is a wall in front of someone who just wants to start |
| D5 | Leaving a solve never asks "are you sure". The solve is kept and Home offers **Carry on** | Forgiving beats confirming: an accidental back gesture with a cube in both hands costs one tap to undo |
| D6 | Advancing a turn: a tap anywhere on the cube or on a bar a third of the screen high, or any of Space, Enter, Page Down, the forward arrow. A paced timer is the option. Voice and camera-follow are later | The tap ships with what exists and works with a knuckle or a page-turner pedal. See `screens/solve.md` for the comparison |
| D7 | Scanning with the camera never saves a picture in a release build. R4's automatic recording of failed scans is a developer-build tool | It is the only reading under which "nothing is stored" stays true, and those frames show whoever is holding the cube. The other outcome is designed in `screens/privacy.md` in case the scan lane needs it |
| D8 | Size classes are compact (under 600), medium (600-839) and expanded (840 and over), with a short class for heights under 480 | Replaces the single 720 breakpoint. Short is a phone on its side, which is how many people will prop it |
| D9 | First run is one screen, not a carousel | People skip carousels. Three numbered steps and one button fit on a phone |
| D10 | Source strings are British English, matching the app today ("colour", "anticlockwise", "licence"). US English is a ninth, cheap translation if wanted | Open: Iain to say whether `en_US` ships |
| D11 | Text never goes below 13 px at the default size, and body text is 16 px | The audit found 29 sizes of 11 px or less |

## Open questions for Iain

1. **D7** - is failed-scan recording developer-only? It is the scan lane's
   call as much as this one's.
2. **D10** - ship `en_US` as well?
3. **Reading turns aloud.** Qt has text-to-speech on all five platforms and
   it runs on the device. It would serve hands-full use and people who
   cannot see the picture. It adds the Qt Speech module. Proposed as a
   setting, off by default; say if it is in or out of the first release.
4. **Package identifier.** Android and iOS need a reverse-domain id that
   will never change. `org.qtproject.example` is what is there now.
5. **CJK fonts.** Japanese and Chinese fonts are 5-10 MB each. The proposal
   is to use the system's own, which every target OS ships except minimal
   Linux installs. See `platforms.md`.
