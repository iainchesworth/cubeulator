**Another size of cube.** Home has a new button, *Another size of cube*. Choose 2×2 to 5×5, type or paste the scramble that mixed the cube up (in the notation timers and puzzle sites use, such as `R U 2R' Fw`), and follow the solution turn by turn on a cube drawn at that size. Each turn says which layer to move, and a cube bigger than a 3×3 first pairs up its centres and edges, which you can skip if you have done that yourself. The camera still reads only a 3×3.
