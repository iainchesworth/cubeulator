**Sides with near-black stickers are read more reliably.** Under a warm lamp a real cube's green and blue read almost black, and beside a white or yellow sticker the reader clipped them, so a side with a column or a row of them could be unreadable at the pose it was held in while the same side a quarter-turn away read. A frame the first look cannot make a side of is now looked at again with a narrower threshold, and what it finds is kept only if it sits on dark plastic. Over the recorded sessions 137 more frames read (2.5%), none that read before changed, and the green and blue sides of the warm-lamp session were read up to 2.5 times as often.
