**The scan no longer stalls the browser page as it starts, or grows slower the longer a cube is held.** In a browser the scan runs on the page's one thread, and the first frame of a scan took a quarter of a second of it (more than a second on a phone-class processor) building a colour table; a cube held in view cost the scan a little more every second; and putting six sides together that make no cube searched every turn of them one by one. The table is built while the camera opens, a held cube's colours are kept as running medians, and the search gives up a combination as soon as an edge or a corner of it is impossible, to the same answers.
