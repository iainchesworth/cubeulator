**A logo on a centre sticker no longer tints it.** The white centre of a Rubik's cube carries a small multicoloured logo, and the scan read that tile darker and pinker than the whites around it, most of all by lamplight. Each sticker is now read from a wider patch and takes the colour most of it shares, so the logo is left out and the tile shows as the white it is. See [the vision pipeline](docs/vision-pipeline.md).
