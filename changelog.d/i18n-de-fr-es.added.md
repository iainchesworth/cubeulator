**German, French and Spanish.** Choose Deutsch, Français or Español in Settings and the app reads in that language, with the plural forms each has and numbers and dates in its own format; before this they changed only the layout direction and the language button, and every string stayed English. All three are first drafts made with machine assistance that no fluent reader has been through (as Japanese and Simplified Chinese are): expect wording a native speaker would change, and tell us where. The app does not say so itself yet. See [docs/i18n.md](docs/i18n.md) for the words a reader should check first.
