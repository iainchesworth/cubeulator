**Type a cube in, a colour at a time.** On *Another size of cube*, *Enter the colours* opens a screen to paint a cube of any size 2×2 to 5×5 side by side as you hold it, for a cube you have no scramble for. It counts the colours as you go, says which colour is over or short, says when the cube is one no turns could have made (and which piece looks wrong), and solves it when it is a whole, possible cube.
