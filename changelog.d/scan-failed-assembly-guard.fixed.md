**A scan whose six sides do not make a cube no longer works the same answer out again for every frame.** When the sides that were read do not fit together - a misread side, or colours that drifted - the scan put them together afresh each time the camera moved a colour by a hair, which took up to about a tenth of a second per frame on a computer. It now does so again only when what was read has changed, and gives the same answer at the same frame as before. See [the vision pipeline](docs/vision-pipeline.md).
