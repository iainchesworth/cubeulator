**The scan says which corner to show when a cube is ambiguous.** When the six sides you showed fit together in more than one way - patterned cubes do - the screen no longer only asks for "two sides at once": it names the corner whose view would settle it, by the colours of the three sides that meet there, such as "Turn the cube slowly to show the corner where Yellow, Green and Red meet".
