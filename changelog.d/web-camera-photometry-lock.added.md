**The browser version holds the camera's exposure and white balance for a scan, where the camera allows it.** A scan reads the same sticker on every side, and a camera's own exposure and white balance re-meter whenever a different colour fills the frame, so a colour could change between one side and the next. In Chrome and Edge the app now asks a camera that offers it to hold both where they settled, checks that it did, and gives them back when the scan ends; a browser or camera that does not offer it (Firefox and Safari do not) keeps its own automatics as before, and the scan goes on either way. What the camera allowed is in the [diagnostics](docs/diagnostics.md) record, and [docs/web.md](docs/web.md) says what differs from the desktop apps.
