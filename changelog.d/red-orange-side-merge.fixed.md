**Two sides that look alike are no longer read as one.** A partly solved cube can have two sides that differ only in which stickers are red and which are orange. Under a warm lamp those two colours are close, and the scan took both sides for one, so it kept swapping red and orange as you turned the cube and never reached six sides. A side is now told by its centre sticker, and a side turned a quarter-turn is laid down by the stickers that tell the turns apart, so the scan completes.
