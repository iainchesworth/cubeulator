**A side turned to the edge of what the scan reads square-on is read.** In a cube turned slowly between two sides, one side spends part of every quarter-turn at about thirty degrees while the other is too thin to see. The reader laid a square grid on the stickers first, which is wrong for a side that is narrower one way or sheared, so a side inside the documented limit (a column step 0.85 of a row's, a cosine of 0.17) was read only up to about 0.87. When no side is held square it now places the stickers again, through the lattice they make, and refuses a fit that overlaps a better one that is not a side; a side that was read is read as before. On a drawn tumble 36 of 40 frames in that band read, up from 21; on the five recorded sessions it gains 11 reads and loses none, and the cube is known at the same frame.
