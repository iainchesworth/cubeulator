**Put right what the camera misread.** Once six sides are in, *Check the colours* on the scan screen opens the colours it read, one side at a time, instead of making you scan again. A "?" marks a sticker the camera was not sure of; pick a colour and tap or drag over the stickers that are wrong, or *Describe this side* to type a whole side as letters (`WWW/WRW/WWW`). *Undo*, *Redo* and *Back to the scan* take changes back, *Turn this side* fixes a side that came the wrong way up, and the screen says when what is shown cannot be a real cube and points at the piece that looks wrong. Solve stays off until it can.
